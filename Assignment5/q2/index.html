The City of Taj Mahal

History

Agra is one of the most famous cities in India, known for the Taj Mahal and Mughal heritage.

Famous Places

Taj Mahal, Agra Fort, Fatehpur Sikri and Mehtab Bagh are major attractions.

Famous Food

Agra is famous for Petha, Bedai, Jalebi and Mughlai cuisine.
