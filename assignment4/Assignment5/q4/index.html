Importance of Technology in Education

By Ritik kushwaha | 4 September 2026

Technology has transformed the way students learn and teachers teach. It makes education more interactive and accessible.

Online classes, digital libraries, and educational apps help students study anytime and anywhere.

Modern classrooms use smart boards, videos, and presentations to improve understanding and learning.

Education is the most powerful weapon to change the world.

Technology improves creativity, communication, and problem-solving skills.
